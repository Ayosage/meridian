# Meridian client: UX/UI critique (2026-09-10)

Method: dual-agent Impeccable critique. Assessment A (design director review of
source + 16 screenshots) and Assessment B (detector run, hand CSS/markup audit,
collision geometry) ran as isolated subagents; this file is the synthesis.
Evidence gathered live on a 4-player / 3-bot match at 1440x900, 1280x720 and
390x844 with the dev client on :5180 and server on :2570 (both stopped after).
Raw assessments: session scratchpad `meridian/assessment-A.md`, `assessment-B.md`.
Screenshots referenced by number were captured the same session.

Detector: `impeccable detect` returned 0 findings. Probe showed the engine in
regex mode only flags font and palette clichés, so "clean" here means no
Inter/Geist and no purple gradients. Everything below came from hand audit.

## Design specificity verdict

**Split product.** The board is authored for this game and nothing else: painted
diorama tiles, carved port signs, sail boats, a physical dice canvas with its
own eased tumble. The HUD and lobby are category-interchangeable: every panel is
`rgba(12,14,20,0.8)` at 8px radius in the system sans, one teal accent, zero
transitions, zero focus or press states, zero media queries. Swap the board for
a chess set and no HUD pixel would change. The lobby ships browser-default
buttons (`#efefef`, UA focus ring) as the first thing a portfolio visitor sees.

Detector agreed by omission (no cliché fonts or palettes). The two assessments
converged independently on: the lobby's UA chrome, the setup-phase clutter, the
absent motion layer, the sub-12px type, the waiting-room dead end, and the win
modal hiding the board.

## Overall impression

The first look at the board and the first placement are the strongest beats in
the product and the code behind them is careful. Everything that brackets them
(lobby, waiting room, end screen) and everything that sits on top of the board
(HUD chrome, copy, motion) has not had the same attention. A visitor who only
sees the lobby and the win modal sees the least designed surfaces in the
project. The single biggest opportunity: make the interface feel like it
belongs to the object underneath it, and give players a way in and a way out.

## What's working

1. The board is a real object. Port signs with carved numerals, a physical
   dice canvas, pulsing legal-target markers shared through one material.
2. Error prevention is thorough and quiet. Unaffordable actions are disabled,
   steppers cap at hand, discard locks at exact N, bank trade rewrites its
   button to the actual deal ("Trade 4 wood → 1 ore"), Join waits for 4 chars.
3. Costs live on the buttons as icon rows straight from the rules' `COSTS`
   table, removing the most common Catan recall burden.
4. The game-semantic colors (resources, seats) have one TS source shared by
   the 3D scene and the HUD (`palette.ts`), and `ResourceIcon` is properly
   labelled for assistive tech.

## Priority issues

**P0. Waiting room has no exit and no invite.** `WaitingRoom.tsx` renders the
code, seats and "waiting for players (1/4)". No leave, no copy code, no invite
link (the `?join=CODE` route already works). A host who picked 4 humans by
accident is stuck until they clear sessionStorage. The code panel is also
absolutely positioned at (12,12), detached from the centered column (C13).
Fix: Leave (wired to the existing `leaveCatanMatch`), Copy code, Copy invite
link, the code centered at display size, an always-visible host control with
its enabling condition stated. `harden`.

**P0. Disconnects and dead links fail silently.** `net/catan.ts` sets the toast
"connection lost" then `status: 'error'`; `App.tsx` renders `<Lobby />` for
error, and Lobby only reads its own local error, so the message never shows.
`status === 'reconnecting'` has no UI: the last view stays up frozen. Rule
errors surface as `${code}: ${message}` in a 13px pill for 3s at bottom
center. Fix: a connection strip mounted in both Lobby and HUD reading status
and toast; "Reconnecting…" over the board; distinct "code not found" vs
"server unreachable"; drop the code prefix from toasts and anchor them near
the action. `clarify` + `harden`.

**P1. Lobby is browser-default and unlabeled.** Twelve UA buttons in three
visual treatments, a placeholder-only input with no `<label>`, no group labels
or `aria-pressed`, no heading for either row, no sentence about the game, bot
row reflows and shifts "Create match" when the player count changes. Fix:
style the lobby with the HUD tokens at minimum; two labelled fieldsets
("Players", "Fill empty seats with bots"); split "Create a match" from "Join
with a code"; one line of context and a "How to play" affordance. `onboard`.

**P1. Setup phase shows nine disabled controls for one live affordance.** Build
bar (five disabled, Settlement highlighted while disabled), Roll, END TURN,
dice "—", four zeroed player cards, five "bank 19" lines. The only hint that
would help ("drag to rotate · scroll to zoom") is 11px at 55% opacity with no
backing, sitting on open water and touching the dice panel (C1). Fix: gate
the build bar, Roll and END TURN on `phase !== 'setup'`; one instruction card
under the banner ("Click a glowing corner to place your settlement"); hide
bank counts until the first roll. `distill`.

**P1. Bots lose their identity between rooms.** The waiting room says
"Bot 1 / 2 / 3"; in-match the same seats are "Player 2 / 3 / 4" on cards, in
the banner and on the offer card, with no marker. Fix: carry the bot label
through `seatNames` or render a badge from `botCount`. `clarify`.

**P1. The ending is a dialog box.** `WinOverlay` is a 280px modal over a 55%
backdrop with one button; no standings, no rematch, board hidden. And because
no `z-index` is declared anywhere, BuildBar, TradePanel, IncomingOffer and
DevCardStrip (later siblings in `App.tsx`) paint above the win backdrop and
stay clickable (C11). Fix: an end card with per-seat VP and awards, "Play
again" and "Back to lobby", board visible with a slow orbit; a stacking
context for overlays. `layout` + `animate`.

**P2. No motion, focus, or press states anywhere in the HUD.** `hud.css` has
zero `transition`, `animation`, `:active`, `:focus-visible`, `@media`. One
`:hover` rule, ungated. Turn changes, offers, toasts, modals cut in a single
frame; the offer card lands on the dice readout (C2/C3). Fix: a small motion
layer (opacity + 4px translate enter at ~200ms on `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)`,
`scale(0.97)` on `:active`, a themed `:focus-visible` ring, reduced-motion
keeps opacity), and pulse the active player card on turn change. `animate`.

**P2. Type and targets are under the floor; nothing adapts.** 18 declarations
under 12px (9px `.badge-new`, 10px `.bank-value` at 0.5 opacity, 10px
`.build-cost`, 11px log). `.bank-value` fails AA over a lit board. Every
pressable except the four build buttons with cost rows is under 44px (log
toggle 17px, steppers 22px and 28px, tabs 24px). `#fff` on the green Roll and
Accept buttons is 3.9:1. There is no breakpoint: at 390 the build bar is
558px wide, covers Roll and END TURN, and the right rail covers the banner
(C5-C7); at 1280x720 an open log will meet an open trade panel (C4). Fix: 12px
floor, 13px for anything read during play, opacity no lower than 0.7 on
text, 44px targets, one breakpoint near 1100px that collapses the rail and
wraps the build bar. `typeset` + `adapt`.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey (short)

- **Lobby**: twelve buttons, one input, zero labels; a six-way choice with no
  question attached; create and join read as a sequence, not an either-or.
- **Waiting room**: the one decision the host needs ("how do I get my friend
  in?") has no control. The rule for "Start now" (`seats == target-1 && bots == 0`)
  is unpredictable from the outside.
- **Setup**: banner says what, board shows where, nothing says how. After
  END TURN with bots the banner freezes on someone else's name with no
  activity signal.
- **Robber on a 7**: discard modal ("Discard 4 cards", no "because a 7 was
  rolled"), then a hex click, then a steal modal, with no narrative thread.
- **Trade**: composer is fine; "You get" has no cap and no opponent hint; the
  bank tab's rewriting submit is the best copy in the app.
- **End**: the climax of a game whose selling point is the board hides the
  board. Peak is the first placement; the end is a dialog.

## Minor observations

Em dashes in nine UI strings (banner variants, lobby error, offer footnote,
monopoly modal, log). Unicode glyphs as icons (`▸`, `×`, `−`, `→`). `END TURN`
caps vs sentence-case siblings; lowercase sentence starts in status lines.
Placeholder "C O D E" off-center from trailing letter-spacing. Five alphas of
the same panel black and three of the same hairline blue; radii 6/8/10/12 with
no rule. Port sign renders through the build bar glass. No `aria-live` on the
turn banner, dice or toast; modals lack `role="dialog"`, focus trap, Escape.
`.banner` in hud.css is dead. `<title>` static, no favicon, no meta description.
Selection, caret, scrollbar and focus ring are browser defaults.

## Recommended order

1. `harden` waiting room: Leave, Copy code, Copy invite link, host start rule stated. (P0)
2. `clarify` + `harden` connection and errors: reconnecting strip, distinct join errors, toast without codes. (P0)
3. `distill` setup phase: hide dead controls, one instruction card, orbit hint with a backing. (P1)
4. `onboard` lobby: labelled fieldsets, split create/join, one line of context, HUD tokens on the controls. (P1)
5. `clarify` bot identity through the match. (P1)
6. `layout` + `animate` end of game: standings, rematch, board visible, overlay stacking fixed. (P1)
7. `animate` motion layer + focus/press states. (P2)
8. `typeset` + `adapt`: 12px floor, 44px targets, Roll/Accept contrast, one breakpoint. (P2)

Items 1, 2, 3 and 5 are small and mechanical. 4 and 6 are design work. 7 and 8
touch every panel and should come after the chrome has a token layer.

## Provocative questions

1. If a hiring manager only sees the lobby and the win screen, what does
   Meridian look like to them?
2. Should the HUD be made of the same material as the board (wood, paper,
   paint, a display face), or is a clean instrument panel over a diorama the
   intended contrast? Either answer works; the current HUD is neither.
3. What is the end of a game for? Standings, a rematch and a reacting board
   say "an evening with friends"; a 280px modal says "the fixture reached
   target VP".
